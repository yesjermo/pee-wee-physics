# HydroSim → Game Roadmap

Saved 2026-11-08 so the simulator can be turned into a game later.

## What already exists (simulator.html)
- Modular human figure (lathed torso/limbs, head, joints) with pixel-mosaic censor, X-ray + cross-section
- Bladder physiology: fill rate from hydration + diuretics, pressure → flow → exit speed
- 15 food/drink items that change hydration and urine color (pigments decay over time)
- Stream controls: aim, power (distance), duration timer, trajectory preview, predicted range
- Toilet bowl fills (1200 mL) → must flush → overflow floods floor → flood dissolves you into a Pee Ball
- Overdrive mode drains body water → Pee Ball; Respawn
- 5 scenarios: bathroom, park, campfire, snow (snowman), slime range
- Targets: slimes, Gloop monsters, cans, rubber ducks, cones, campfire, snowman; auto-aim + score
- Procedural Web Audio (stream hiss, ceramic tap, water plink, tile patter, sizzle) with HRTF spatial audio

## Game ideas to build next
1. **Wave survival mode:** slimes/Gloops come in waves; manage bladder + drinks as ammo
2. **Hydration economy:** drinks are pickups; salty food is a trap; overdrive is a risky power-up
3. **Toilet-keeper mode:** timed levels where you must hit the bowl and flush before overflow
4. **Score & combo system:** streak multipliers, accuracy %, high-score table (localStorage)
5. **Player controls:** WASD walking, mouse aim, gamepad support
6. **Levels:** campfire rescue, snowman art (draw shapes in snow), carnival target booth
7. **Pee Ball mode:** after dissolving, roll the ball around as a bonus mini-game
8. **Polish:** title screen, pause menu, settings, sound for slime pops, mobile touch controls

## Content rules kept so far
- No human (or little people) targets; no real animals; only original characters (no copyrighted ones)
- Genitals are never modelled, only a pixel censor
