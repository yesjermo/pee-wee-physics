What's inside

Physics, physiology and procedural sound in one viewport

360° Spatial Audio & Surface Acoustics

Procedurally generated fluid stream audio and dynamic splash impact sounds tied to camera orientation. Pink noise, band-pass filters and LFOs run through HRTF panners anchored to the exit point and impact zone.

  • → Zero audio files: 100% synthesized
  • → Ceramic tap · water plink · tile patter

Dynamic Morphing & Bladder Volume

Scale belly size, adjust water intake, and watch pressure drive stream trajectory and audio pitch. Bladder radius follows real volume (up to 500 mL) and flow peaks near a typical 24 mL/s.

  • → Detrusor pressure in cmH₂O
  • → Ballistic auto-aim at slimes, cans & fire
  • → Overdrive until you melt into a pee ball

Substance & Color Spectrum

Real-time visual shading driven by simulated intake (Water, B-Vitamins, Beets, Dehydration). One shader uniform tints the stream, splash droplets, bladder contents and the bowl water.

Sound & physics controls

These controls drive the live viewport

Every switch below talks to the 3D scene above over postMessage. Change the surface and the splash timbre changes; raise water intake and the hue pales while the bladder fills faster.

  1. 01 Pink-noise source → band-pass filter, its frequency swept by an LFO
  2. 02 Gain, cutoff and playback rate scale with bladder pressure
  3. 03 Particle collisions trigger per-surface impact voices
  4. 04 Both emitters are PositionalAudio nodes on the camera's listener
